# Stop Doing This: 5 Phone Charging Habits Destroying Your Battery

Your phone battery is dying faster than it should. And it's probably your fault.

But not for the reasons you think.

I've spent weeks talking to battery engineers and reading lithium-ion research papers so you don't have to. Here's what's actually killing your battery—and the simple fixes.

## Myth #1: You Should Let Your Phone Die Completely

**Wrong.** This was true for old nickel-cadmium batteries. Modern lithium-ion batteries **hate** deep discharges.

Letting your phone hit 0% puts chemical stress on the battery. Do this regularly, and you'll lose capacity permanently.

**The Truth:** Keep your phone between **20% and 80%** for optimal longevity. One full discharge per month is fine for calibration. Daily? You're destroying it.

## Myth #2: Overnight Charging Ruins Your Battery

This one's complicated.

Modern phones have built-in charging circuits that stop at 100%. **However**, keeping a battery at 100% for 8 hours every night creates "trickle charge" stress.

**The Fix:** 
- iPhone: Turn on **Optimized Battery Charging** (Settings &gt; Battery)
- Android: Use **Adaptive Battery** or a charging alarm app
- Better yet: Charge before bed, unplug at 80%

## Myth #3: All Chargers Are the Same

That $3 gas station charger? It might be frying your phone.

Cheap chargers often lack proper voltage regulation. They can deliver "dirty" power that heats up your battery—and heat is lithium-ion's worst enemy.

**The Rule:** Use the charger that came with your phone, or a certified replacement (MFi for iPhone, USB-IF for Android). The $15 you save on a cheap charger could cost you a $500 battery replacement.

## Myth #4: Wireless Charging Is "Safe" for Your Battery

Wireless charging generates **more heat** than wired charging. Heat accelerates battery aging.

A 2021 study found batteries charged wirelessly degraded **15-20% faster** over two years compared to wired charging.

**The Fix:** 
- Remove thick cases during wireless charging
- Don't wireless charge in hot environments (cars in summer, direct sunlight)
- Use wired charging for your daily top-up; save wireless for convenience

## Myth #5: Closing Apps Saves Battery

This is the biggest lie in smartphone history.

iOS and Android are designed to freeze background apps. When you "close" them, they don't stay closed—they **restart from scratch** next time you open them. That restart uses more power than leaving them frozen.

**The Exception:** Apps with background GPS (Uber, Maps) or music streaming. Those you should close.

**For everything else:** Let the OS handle it.

## The Optimal Charging Routine

Based on everything I learned, here's the ideal daily habit:

1. **Morning:** Charge to 80% while getting ready
2. **Midday:** Top up for 20 minutes if you hit 30%
3. **Evening:** Charge to 80% before bed, unplug
4. **Avoid:** Heat, 0% drains, cheap chargers, overnight 100%

## When to Replace Your Battery

- iPhone: Battery Health under 80% (Settings &gt; Battery)
- Android: AccuBattery app shows capacity
- General rule: If you're charging twice a day, it's time

A $69 battery replacement beats a $1,000 new phone.
